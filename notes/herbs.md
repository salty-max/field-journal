## 2447 Peacebloom

The first flower most herbalists ever pick, and the one they never stop seeing. Peacebloom grows in the meadows of every starting land: Elwynn and Dun Morogh, Teldrassil and Darkshore, Durotar, Mulgore and the Barrens, Tirisfal and Silverpine.

Ground with Silverleaf it makes the minor healing draught every apprentice alchemist brews by the dozen, and it steadies a weak troll's blood potion. Common, humble, and in every satchel that matters.

## 765 Silverleaf

A low herb with leaves the colour of old coin, found wherever Peacebloom is: the fields of Elwynn and Westfall, the hills of Loch Modan, the red earth of Durotar and the Barrens, the plains of Mulgore.

It is the other half of the minor healing potion, and with Mageroyal it makes the minor mana potion. An alchemist's first lesson is that Silverleaf goes into nearly everything, and runs out first.

## 2449 Earthroot

A thick brown root that pushes up out of the soil like a knuckle. It keeps company with Peacebloom and Silverleaf across the young lands, from Elwynn and Redridge to Durotar, Mulgore and the Barrens, and it has been found growing in the Wailing Caverns.

Earthroot is a root of strength: the Elixir of Lion's Strength begins with it, and so do the Elixir of Minor Fortitude and the Elixir of Ogre's Strength.

## 785 Mageroyal

A pale, proud flower that prefers a little more wilderness than Peacebloom: the Barrens, Ashenvale and Darkshore in Kalimdor, Westfall, Silverpine, Redridge, Duskwood and the Wetlands beyond.

As its name promises, it is a flower of the mind. The minor and lesser mana potions are made from it, and so is the Elixir of Wisdom. Look about a Mageroyal for Swiftthistle, which grows in its shade.

## 2450 Briarthorn

A tangle of thorned stems that snags cloaks and fingers alike. Briarthorn grows through the Barrens, Ashenvale and Stonetalon, and across the sea in Westfall, Duskwood, Redridge, the Wetlands and the Hillsbrad Foothills. The quilboar keep it in Razorfen Kraul.

It is a fighter's herb: the lesser healing potion and the healing potion use it, as do the Rage Potion and the Swiftness Potion.

## 2452 Swiftthistle

A small, spiny herb that grows nowhere on its own: it is found in the shade of Mageroyal and Briarthorn, and gathered with them by the herbalist who looks closely.

Swiftthistle is the herb of haste. It goes into the Swiftness Potion, the Swim Speed Potion and the elixirs of agility, and the rogues of every city brew their Thistle Tea from it.

## 2453 Bruiseweed

A dark, broad-leaved weed named for the colour of its leaves and the use of its sap. It is one of the most widespread herbs of the middle lands: Stonetalon, the Barrens, Ashenvale, the Thousand Needles and Desolace; Redridge, Duskwood, the Wetlands and the Hillsbrad Foothills. It grows in Blackfathom Deeps and the Wailing Caverns too.

Bruiseweed goes into the healing potion, the strong troll's blood potion and the Elixir of Poison Resistance.

## 3820 Stranglekelp

A sea plant, gathered from the shallows rather than the fields: along the coasts of Stranglethorn, Westfall, the Wetlands and the Hillsbrad Foothills, and on the shores of Darkshore, Ashenvale, the Barrens, Desolace and Feralas. Expect to hold your breath for it.

It makes the Elixir of Water Breathing, and goes into the lesser mana potion, the mana potion and the Free Action Potion.

## 3355 Wild Steelbloom

A hard, grey-green flower whose stem resists a knife. It favours dry slopes and rocky ground: the Arathi Highlands, the Badlands, Alterac, Stonetalon and the Thousand Needles, and the hills of Stranglethorn, the Wetlands and the Barrens.

Steelbloom is the alchemist's armour: the Elixir of Defense and the Elixir of Fortitude are made from it.

## 3369 Grave Moss

A pale moss that grows where the dead lie: in Duskwood, the Wetlands, Arathi and Alterac, in the bone-strewn wastes of Desolace, and in the halls of Razorfen Downs.

It is the stuff of shadow. The Shadow Protection Potion and Shadow Oil are made from it, and few herbalists linger over picking it.

## 3356 Kingsblood

A deep red flower whose name suggests an old story and whose colour suggests the rest. It grows widely in the middle lands: Stranglethorn, the Wetlands, the Hillsbrad Foothills, Arathi, Alterac and Duskwood; Ashenvale, Stonetalon, the Barrens, Desolace and the Thousand Needles.

Kingsblood is one of the alchemist's great workhorses: the greater healing potion, the mana potion, the Elixir of Firepower and the Great Rage Potion all call for it, and leatherworkers use it for Herbalist's Gloves.

## 3357 Liferoot

A pale root that grows where its feet stay wet: riverbanks, marsh edges and lake shores in Stranglethorn, the Wetlands, Arathi, Alterac, the Hillsbrad Foothills, the Hinterlands and the Swamp of Sorrows, and in Dustwallow, Feralas, Desolace and Ashenvale.

It goes into the greater healing potion and the mighty troll's blood potion, and gives the Elixir of Waterwalking its name's worth.

## 3818 Fadeleaf

A grey herb that seems to fade into its own shadow, and grows in shadowed places: Arathi, Alterac, the Swamp of Sorrows, Stranglethorn, the Hinterlands, the Badlands and Dustwallow. It has been found in Razorfen Kraul and the Scarlet Monastery.

Fadeleaf is the herb of not being seen. It makes the Lesser Invisibility Potion and the Elixir of Detect Lesser Invisibility, and the rogues grind it into Blinding Powder.

## 3821 Goldthorn

A thorny shrub with a golden cast to its stems, common in the harder lands: Stranglethorn, Arathi, Alterac, the Swamp of Sorrows, the Hinterlands, the Badlands and Feralas.

Goldthorn goes into the greater mana potion, the Elixir of Agility, the Elixir of Fortitude and the Frost Protection Potion.

## 3358 Khadgar's Whisker

A wiry, pale herb that bears the name of the archmage Khadgar. It grows in Stranglethorn, Arathi, Alterac, the Swamp of Sorrows, the Hinterlands and the Badlands, and in Feralas and Dustwallow.

Fitting its name, it is a mage's herb: the greater mana potion, the Elixir of Greater Intellect, the Elixir of Frost Power and Frost Oil are made from it.

## 3819 Wintersbite

A small blue-white flower that grows in one place only: the snows of the Alterac Mountains. It is cold to the touch even in the hand.

Wintersbite is the heart of frost alchemy: Frost Oil, the Elixir of Frost Power and the Frost Protection Potion all need it.

## 8153 Wildvine

A green creeper found twined about Purple Lotus, and gathered with it: in Azshara, the Hinterlands, Tanaris, Feralas, Stranglethorn and Ashenvale.

Unusually, the alchemists want it less than the crafters do: leatherworkers use it for their wild leather armour, and tailors for dreamweave.

## 4625 Firebloom

A flower that only grows where the ground is hot: the Searing Gorge, the Badlands and the dunes of Tanaris. It is warm to the touch, and dry as tinder.

It is the stuff of fire: the Elixir of Greater Firepower, Wizard Oil and Oil of Immolation are made from it, engineers want it for Goblin Rocket Fuel, and it is one of the herbs of the Philosophers' Stone.

## 8831 Purple Lotus

A broad lotus of the warm lands: Azshara, the Hinterlands, Tanaris, Feralas, Stranglethorn and Ashenvale. The trolls of Zul'Gurub keep it too. Look for Wildvine wound about it.

It is a flower of sleep and dreams: the Dreamless Sleep Potion and the Elixir of Dream Vision are made from it, and it goes into the Philosophers' Stone.

## 8836 Arthas' Tears

A drooping white flower that bears the name of the prince who brought the plague to Lordaeron. It grows in the Western and Eastern Plaguelands and in tainted Felwood, and in the halls of Razorfen Downs, among the dead.

It makes the Elixir of Detect Undead, and Gift of Arthas, which spreads a little of the plague's malice to anyone who strikes the drinker.

## 8838 Sungrass

A golden grass that turns to follow the light, and grows in the bright places of the high lands: Azshara, the Hinterlands, Feralas, Un'Goro, the Burning Steppes, Felwood and both Plaguelands. It grows in Zul'Gurub as well.

Sungrass goes into the superior healing potion, the superior mana potion, the Invisibility Potion and the Elixir of Giants.

## 8839 Blindweed

A pale weed of swamp water, found in the Swamp of Sorrows and in the steaming jungle of Un'Goro, and deep in Maraudon.

It goes into the superior mana potion, the Elixir of Greater Intellect and the Arcane Elixir, and with Arthas' Tears into Gift of Arthas.

## 8845 Ghost Mushroom

A luminous fungus that grows only in darkness, in the caves of the Hinterlands and Desolace, and in Maraudon and Dire Maul.

It is a herb of the unseen and the unholy: the Invisibility Potion, the Elixir of Shadow Power and the Elixir of Demonslaying need it, and so does Ghost Dye, which tailors buy from alchemists.

## 8846 Gromsblood

A red-black plant that grows where demonic taint has soaked into the soil: Felwood above all, the Blasted Lands below the Dark Portal, the wastes of Desolace, and within Dire Maul.

It is a herb of brute strength: the Elixir of Giants, the Elixir of Brute Force, the Mighty Rage Potion and the Flask of the Titans all need it.

## 13464 Golden Sansam

A rich golden herb of the high and wild lands: Un'Goro, Azshara, Felwood, the Hinterlands, Feralas, the Burning Steppes and the Eastern Plaguelands, and Zul'Gurub.

It is the herb of the major healing potion and the major rejuvenation potion, and every healer in a long fight is grateful for it.

## 13463 Dreamfoil

A silver-leaved plant of the high and haunted lands, found in Azshara, Un'Goro, Felwood, the Burning Steppes, Silithus and both Plaguelands, and in Dire Maul and Zul'Gurub.

Dreamfoil is the herb of the greater protection potions, against fire, frost, nature, shadow and arcane alike, and of the Greater Arcane Elixir and the Flask of Distilled Wisdom.

## 13465 Mountain Silversage

A hardy silver herb of the heights: Winterspring, Azshara, the Burning Steppes and the Eastern Plaguelands, and, oddly, the crater of Un'Goro. Zul'Gurub has it as well.

It makes the major healing potion and the Greater Arcane Elixir, and with Plaguebloom the Elixir of the Mongoose. Some of the flasks call for it too.

## 13466 Plaguebloom

A sickly flower that thrives on the very blight that killed everything else: it grows only in the Western and Eastern Plaguelands.

Its uses are as strange as its soil. It makes the Purification Potion, the Mageblood Potion and the major troll's blood potion, and with Mountain Silversage the Elixir of the Mongoose.

## 13467 Icecap

A frozen white flower that grows in one land: the snows of Winterspring.

It makes the major mana potion and the Purification Potion, and goes into the Flask of Distilled Wisdom and the Flask of Chromatic Resistance.

## 13468 Black Lotus

The rarest herb in the world, and the one every herbalist hopes to see. Black Lotus grows in only a few dangerous lands: the Burning Steppes, Winterspring, the Eastern Plaguelands and Silithus.

Every great flask needs one: the Flask of the Titans, the Flask of Distilled Wisdom, the Flask of Supreme Power and the Flask of Chromatic Resistance. Those who find one rarely tell anyone where.

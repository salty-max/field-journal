# The naturalist's notes on herbs

## 2447 Peacebloom

Peacebloom is a familiar flower along the first roads out of home, and useful long after an herbalist has learned rarer names.

With Silverleaf it makes a Minor Healing Potion. There is something agreeable about finding the beginnings of a remedy in country where an apprentice can still recognise the way back.

## 765 Silverleaf

Silverleaf belongs in the same early gathering satchel as Peacebloom. A plant need not be rare to become indispensable to the workbench.

Minor healing and mana draughts both call for it. The two uses explain why an alchemist who has gathered plenty can discover, quite shortly, that plenty was not enough.

## 2449 Earthroot

Earthroot turns up across the settled lands and even within the Wailing Caverns. It is one of the first roots an herbalist learns to recognise.

The Elixir of Lion's Strength and the Elixir of Minor Fortitude both begin with it. Such imposing names have modest ingredients, which is a comfort to an apprentice.

## 785 Mageroyal

Mageroyal is a useful find along the roads beyond the first settlements. Swiftthistle is sometimes gathered with it, giving a single stop a second reward.

Alchemists use Mageroyal in mana draughts and the Elixir of Wisdom. The name sets a high expectation; the work it does for a young spellcaster is quite sufficient.

## 2450 Briarthorn

Briarthorn is familiar in the Barrens and the wooded country across the sea. Swiftthistle may come away with the gathered stems.

Healing draughts and Rage Potions both call for it. The same bundle can therefore help somebody recover from a fight or prepare to begin another.

## 2452 Swiftthistle

Swiftthistle comes with Mageroyal and Briarthorn rather than from a patch of its own. An herbalist looking for it must pay attention to what the other gathering yields.

Rogues brew Thistle Tea from it, while alchemists use it for swiftness and swimming draughts.

## 2453 Bruiseweed

Bruiseweed is widespread enough to become a regular part of the gathering round. The Wailing Caverns and Blackfathom Deeps have it too, for those whose errands lead below ground.

Healing Potions and the Elixir of Poison Resistance give it dependable work.

## 3820 Stranglekelp

Stranglekelp takes the herbalist away from the path and into coastal water. The gathering itself makes a good argument for knowing what is growing below the surface.

An Elixir of Water Breathing is one of its uses, along with mana draughts and Free Action Potions. The plant supplies a remedy for an inconvenience encountered while collecting it.

## 3355 Wild Steelbloom

Wild Steelbloom is a familiar name to herbalists working the hills of Arathi and the rocky country of Stonetalon.

The Elixir of Defense and the Elixir of Fortitude both use it. Armour is the smith's business, but the alchemist has a few contributions to make before the traveller buckles it on.

## 3369 Grave Moss

Grave Moss is gathered in places where an herbalist is unlikely to feel entirely alone: Duskwood, Desolace and the burial ground of Razorfen Downs among them.

Shadow Oil and Shadow Protection Potions call for it. The uses are well suited to the errands that bring a gatherer among the graves.

## 3356 Kingsblood

Kingsblood occurs across a wide stretch of country, from wooded hills to the dry interior of Kalimdor. Its value does not depend on a difficult search.

Greater healing draughts and mana potions both use it, and leatherworkers need it for Herbalist's Gloves. A gathering herb that helps make gathering gloves has a pleasing place in the trade.

## 3357 Liferoot

Liferoot is found in wet country on both continents, with the marshes and river lands offering much of the gathering.

Greater Healing Potions and the Elixir of Waterwalking call for it.

## 3818 Fadeleaf

Fadeleaf is gathered through the middle lands, with patches inside the Scarlet Monastery and Razorfen Kraul as well.

Rogues prepare Blinding Powder from it. Alchemists put it into potions for becoming unseen and for detecting what is unseen: the same herb contributes to both sides of a difficult encounter.

## 3821 Goldthorn

Goldthorn earns a place in a working satchel through the variety of its uses. Its gathering country includes Arathi's hills and the forests of Feralas.

Greater mana draughts, agility elixirs and Frost Protection Potions all call for it.

## 3358 Khadgar's Whisker

Khadgar's Whisker has a name a traveller is likely to remember, even before learning where to look for it. The herb occurs in several of the wooded and marshy lands.

Greater Mana Potions and the Elixir of Greater Intellect use it, as does Frost Oil. The archmage's name has found its way onto a very busy shelf.

## 3819 Wintersbite

Wintersbite gives an herbalist a particular journey: into the snow of the Alterac Mountains. A familiar plant is welcome company in country where the paths are easily lost.

Frost Oil, Frost Protection Potions and the Elixir of Frost Power all call for it. The mountain herb has work on either side of the struggle with cold.

## 8153 Wildvine

Wildvine is gathered with Purple Lotus, an extra find in country already worth searching for the flower.

Leatherworkers need it for wild leather and tailors for dreamweave. The flower and its companion find work on quite different benches.

## 4625 Firebloom

Firebloom belongs to the gathering routes through Tanaris, the Badlands and Searing Gorge. None is gentle country in which to stop and fill a satchel.

Goblin Rocket Fuel and Oil of Immolation give a fair impression of its uses. The Philosophers' Stone calls for it too, for alchemists with a different ambition for the same bundle.

## 8831 Purple Lotus

Purple Lotus grows in scattered lands on both continents, and sometimes yields Wildvine with the gathering.

Dreamless Sleep Potions and the Elixir of Dream Vision both use it. An alchemist can offer a night without dreams or a journey made through one, depending on what accompanies the lotus in the vial.

## 8836 Arthas' Tears

Arthas' Tears grows in the Plaguelands and Felwood, and is found within Razorfen Downs. The name is a sombre one to learn among so many ruined fields.

The Elixir of Detect Undead and Gift of Arthas are its two familiar preparations. Neither does much to lighten the associations of a bundle brought back from that country.

## 8838 Sungrass

Sungrass remains a welcome find in such troubled country as Felwood and the Plaguelands, as well as in greener lands farther south.

Superior healing and mana draughts both use it.

## 8839 Blindweed

Blindweed leads a gatherer through the Swamp of Sorrows and Un'Goro, with another supply below ground in Maraudon.

Superior Mana Potions and the Arcane Elixir call for it. In Gift of Arthas it accompanies Arthas' Tears.

## 8845 Ghost Mushroom

Ghost Mushrooms make cave country worth a careful search. The Hinterlands and Desolace have them, with more found inside Maraudon and Dire Maul.

Alchemists use them for invisibility and shadow power, but also make Ghost Dye for tailors. A fungus brought out of the dark can finish its journey as the colour of a garment.

## 8846 Gromsblood

Gromsblood is found through the tainted ground of Felwood, near the Dark Portal and within Dire Maul. The gathering route passes some of the Legion's most enduring marks on the world.

The Flask of the Titans and the Elixir of Brute Force both call for it. So does the Elixir of Demonslaying, a particularly satisfying use for something gathered in such company.

## 13464 Golden Sansam

Golden Sansam is gathered in Un'Goro and Azshara, and through such damaged country as Felwood and the Eastern Plaguelands.

Major healing and rejuvenation draughts use it. The healing supplies brought back from those places have no shortage of work awaiting them.

## 13463 Dreamfoil

Dreamfoil is gathered among Azshara's ruins and within Dire Maul, as well as in the scarred lands around Blackrock.

Greater Protection Potions use it against fire, frost, nature, shadow and arcane harm. The Flask of Distilled Wisdom needs it too.

## 13465 Mountain Silversage

Mountain Silversage is gathered in Winterspring and Un'Goro, despite the very different view from the crater floor.

It goes into Major Healing Potions, and with Plaguebloom makes the Elixir of the Mongoose.

## 13466 Plaguebloom

Plaguebloom is gathered in the two Plaguelands, where finding useful growth amid the ruined fields is a small relief.

Purification Potions and Mageblood Potions both use it; Mountain Silversage accompanies it in the Elixir of the Mongoose. Something gathered in that blighted country can still serve the living.

## 13467 Icecap

Icecap belongs to Winterspring's gathering routes. The snow gives an herbalist an unusually consistent landscape in which to learn its place.

Major Mana Potions and Purification Potions use it, along with the Flask of Distilled Wisdom.

## 13468 Black Lotus

Black Lotus is a rare find in a small number of dangerous lands. A gatherer who spots it has good reason to remember the place.

The great flasks call for it, including those of the Titans and Supreme Power. Many bundles can be replenished on an ordinary round; this one tends to determine when the most ambitious work can begin.

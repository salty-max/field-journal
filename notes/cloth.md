# The naturalist's notes on cloth

## 2589 Linen Cloth

Linen serves humbler needs than a wizard's wardrobe. Stephanie Turner in Stormwind asks for it to clothe the children in her care, many orphaned by the Scourge.

It becomes bolts for tailoring, bandages for first aid and rough dynamite for engineers. A bundle brought home from the road can have a rather different purpose depending on who receives it.

## 2592 Wool Cloth

Wool supplies blankets as well as clothing. In the Stockade, Defias prisoners have torn those blankets into the red bandanas of their gang. Even an ordinary fabric can acquire an unpleasant history.

It becomes bolts for tailoring and bandages for first aid. Engineers also use it in a Target Dummy, giving the same cloth a less troublesome way to draw attention.

## 4306 Silk Cloth

Among the Defias, the cloth of a red bandana indicates its wearer's standing. Silk marks the higher ranks, a useful detail for anyone who would rather observe the gang from a distance.

The loose cloth becomes bandages, while its bolts supply finer tailoring. Engineers put it into Iron Grenades and Goblin Jumper Cables, with rather less regard for appearances.

## 4338 Mageweave Cloth

Mageweave's bolts are woven into dreamweave garments with Wildvine, a plant whose potent, unruly qualities also interest leatherworkers. The fabric has work beyond the ordinary travelling clothes.

It supplies bandages as well, and the loose cloth goes into Nightscape Shoulders and mithril armour. Its name does not reserve the bundle for a spellcaster.

## 14047 Runecloth

The Timbermaw value Runecloth for its fine feel. Meilosh's people took an interest after a visiting night elf wore a cloak made from it. A useful fabric can do a little of the work of an introduction.

It supplies tailoring, runic leather clothing and bandages. Runecloth also lines a paladin's charger's barding, beneath the arcanite plating.

## 14256 Felcloth

Felcloth is carried by demons and satyrs. Impsy in Felwood praises the way it burns the skin and encourages wicked thoughts, which is not the recommendation most travellers seek in a fabric.

Tailors use it in felcloth garments and the Robe of the Void. They can also make it into Mooncloth, giving the same unsettling material a very different future.

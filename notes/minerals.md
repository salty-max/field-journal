## 2770 Copper Ore

Copper is the beginning of much ordinary metalwork, from an apprentice's first tools to the axes and chain belts supplied to Loch Modan's mountaineers.

Its bars combine with tin to make bronze. Rough Stone and the occasional gem come away with the ore, so even a modest copper round can supply more than the forge.

## 2771 Tin Ore

Tin has an important place in bronze. At the furnace, its bars are combined with copper rather than simply replacing the earlier metal.

The ore comes from gathering grounds beyond the first copper country. Moving on to tin gives the apprentice a reason to revisit the old deposits too.

## 2775 Silver Ore

A silver vein is a welcome interruption to a round of copper and tin. The ore still needs smelting before it can go to work.

Smiths make silver rods from the bars, ready for an enchanter to add the runes. Even a tool for magic begins with somebody tending a furnace.

## 2772 Iron Ore

Iron has a second life as steel when its bars are smelted with coal. A miner returning from Arathi or the Badlands may be supplying more than one kind of metalwork.

Enchanters also use the ore itself for gloves that improve mining. There is a pleasing economy in putting a little of the work back into one's hands.

## 2776 Gold Ore

Gold supplies the rods enchanters need for their work. Its bars also form part of the offering asked by the Spectral Chalice beneath Blackrock Mountain.

The ore must first be smelted. Even a tribute accepted by the dead begins with somebody tending a furnace.

## 3858 Mithril Ore

The Mithril Order has made a whole course of study out of this metal. Galvan the Ancient considers it nobler than truesilver, though he admits the other metal is stronger.

Its bars supply weapons and armour, while the ore itself goes into Lesser Stoneshield Potions. Galvan's preferences leave the alchemist with perfectly respectable work to do.

## 7911 Truesilver Ore

Truesilver is stronger and more durable than mithril, an assessment even Galvan the Ancient accepts. His dislike of the metal appears to be a separate matter.

The ore is smelted into bars for fine metalwork. Aquamarine, Citrine and Star Rubies can accompany the gathering, giving a good deposit several uses beyond its metal.

## 10620 Thorium Ore

Thorium is trusted for sturdy work. Tundra MacGrann keeps his meat in a thorium locker that he believes a bear cannot break, though the bear has managed to carry the whole thing away.

Small and rich veins yield the same ore. Alchemists use it in Greater Stoneshield Potions, while its bars supply armour, weapons and engineering work.

## 11370 Dark Iron Ore

Dark Iron is a metal of the Blackrock country, worked into the weapons and armour that bear its name. Ordinary furnaces will not smelt its ore. That work belongs at the Black Forge in Blackrock Depths.

Warlocks also require the ore for a Wheel of the Black March, which directs the energies of a summoning circle. Its usefulness does not end at the anvil.

## 2798 Rethban Ore

Rethban Ore takes its name from its discoverer, Bart Rethban. It is found in the caverns north of Lakeshire, where the gnolls have found reason to gather it too.

Smelted, it is weaker than iron. Its dark colour makes a useful scribes' dye instead. A miner's work can end on the page of a book as readily as on a smith's anvil.

## 3340 Incendicite Ore

Incendicite is found in a cave northwest of Algaz Gate in the Wetlands. It is volatile ore, troublesome to work without an explosion.

There is interest in using it for cannon shells, provided someone can first find a suitable way to smelt it. The proposal still leaves the most uncomfortable part to the experimenter.

## 4278 Lesser Bloodstone Ore

Drywhisker Gorge in the Arathi Highlands has deposits of Lesser Bloodstone. Its low grade has not prevented Krazek in Booty Bay from asking for it.

He offers a repair to a traveller's cooking pot in return. An expedition's mineral specimens do not always end in a grander piece of equipment.

## 5833 Indurium Ore

Indurium Ore is mined inside Uldaman. Acquiring it calls for a journey into the ruins, beyond the deposits beside the open road.

The ore supplies work on parts for a race. A material brought out of an ancient excavation can have a surprisingly hurried destination.

## 2835 Rough Stone

Copper mining leaves a great deal of Rough Stone in the bags. Throwing it away is a tempting way to make room for the copper.

Smiths turn it into grinding, sharpening and weight stones. Engineers grind it into blasting powder. Either trade can offer a reason to reconsider the rubbish pile.

## 2836 Coarse Stone

Coarse Stone accompanies tin, easily overlooked when the same gathering turns up a gem.

Smiths use it for grinding and sharpening stones. Engineers make blasting powder from it. The less celebrated part of the load may prove the louder one.

## 2838 Heavy Stone

Heavy Stone comes away with iron ore. A miner working those deposits will soon have enough to interest more than one trade.

It supplies grinding stones for the smith and blasting powder for the engineer.

## 7912 Solid Stone

Solid Stone accompanies mithril. Looking only for the metal and gems leaves part of the deposit's usefulness behind.

Its grinding stones, sharpening stones and blasting powder have their places at the workbench. They seldom receive the attention given to the finished blade or device.

## 12365 Dense Stone

Thorium mining brings Dense Stone to the surface alongside the ore. It is worth keeping even when a gem has claimed everyone's attention.

Besides the smith's usual stones and the engineer's powder, it goes into Elemental Sharpening Stones. A good edge can owe something to the rubble.

## 22202 Small Obsidian Shard

Small Obsidian Shards can come from either size of obsidian chunk. The label on the deposit is no substitute for examining what has broken away.

Smiths use them in light and heavy obsidian belts, as well as in larger works. These pieces have a use before a miner finds a single large shard.

## 22203 Large Obsidian Shard

Large Obsidian Shards are found in the same kinds of chunks as the smaller pieces. Both deserve a line in the mineral account.

They go into the Jagged Obsidian Shield and Thick Obsidian Breastplate. Those patterns call for small shards as well, so the less impressive fragments still belong in the load.

## 774 Malachite

Malachite is among the gems a miner can find while still working copper. An early find can be useful long after the first tools have been replaced.

Engineers use it for a Mechanical Squirrel and a Crude Scope. The same little stone can help someone see farther or provide something small to follow them around.

## 818 Tigerseye

Tigerseye turns up with copper, giving an apprentice something to bring home besides metal and Rough Stone.

Flying Tiger Goggles and a Heavy Copper Broadsword both use it. The miner need not have travelled far to supply someone else's first equipment.

## 1210 Shadowgem

Copper, tin and silver veins can all yield Shadowgems. A familiar gathering round may turn up more than one.

They go into Shadow Goggles and a Runed Silver Rod. The engineer and the enchanter have quite different uses for the same find.

## 1206 Moss Agate

Moss Agate has a place in magical jewellery as well as in a miner's gem pouch. Nogg uses it with silver to strengthen the enchantment of a ring.

Tin and silver veins can yield it. Engineers also fit it into scopes, goggles and an Ornate Spyglass. A small stone can repay more than one kind of close examination.

## 1705 Lesser Moonstone

Tin, silver, gold and iron can all yield Lesser Moonstone. The word lesser has done little to shorten its list of uses.

The Moonsteel Broadsword and Moonsight Rifle both call for it. Their names are rather more elevated than the circumstances in which the stone is usually found.

## 1529 Jade

Jade is among the materials Tabetha asks for when making a mage's wand. Such work calls for a vessel that can hold a considerable store of magical energy.

The gem also goes into a Jade Serpentblade and a Gnomish Cloaking Device. It has found work with people who wish to be noticed, and people who would rather avoid it.

## 3864 Citrine

Galvan the Ancient values Citrine for more than its appearance. He teaches that the gem amplifies the enchantments placed in crafted goods, using it with mithril in his lessons.

It also supplies the tailor's Star Belt and the engineer's Craftsman's Monocle. A jewelled piece need not owe all its usefulness to the metal around the stone.

## 7909 Aquamarine

Aquamarine can emerge from iron, mithril and truesilver deposits. A round of mining supplies more than the metals named along the route.

It is used in Truesilver Gauntlets and an enchantment that lends a little speed to boots. There are several ways for a stone to improve the next journey.

## 7910 Star Ruby

Star Rubies have uses beyond ornament. They form part of the tribute accepted by the Spectral Chalice in Blackrock Depths, alongside gold and truesilver.

Engineers use them in a Sniper Scope and a Mithril Mechanical Dragonling. The League's naturalists have fewer reservations about examining the latter than its living relatives.

## 9262 Black Vitriol

Black Vitriol can accompany mithril, thorium and Dark Iron. It is another reason to inspect what came away with the ore.

[classic] Alchemists need it for a Philosophers' Stone. The miner supplies an ingredient for transmutation without having to explain the rest of the process.

[forever] Alchemists need it for a Philosopher's Stone. The miner supplies an ingredient for transmutation without having to explain the rest of the process.

## 11382 Blood of the Mountain

Blood of the Mountain is a gem found in Dark Iron deposits. The name gives the mineral collector a fair sense of where the search will lead.

It goes into the Smoking Heart of the Mountain and the Sulfuron Hammer. Neither commission does much to soften the associations of the place.

## 11754 Black Diamond

Black Diamonds are among the Dark Iron miner's finds beneath Blackrock. Mathredis Firestar also requires them in the work of turning old librams into lesser arcana.

The stones have a place in Mongoose Boots and Darkrune Helms as well. Not every diamond is destined to be admired on a finger.

## 12361 Blue Sapphire

Blue Sapphires are among the gems brought out of thorium deposits.

Storm Gauntlets and the Lionheart Helm both call for them. The finished armour has less obvious need of a gem than a necklace does, but the smith's recipe is quite firm on the matter.

## 12363 Arcane Crystal

An Arcane Crystal is useful to the alchemist as a means of changing metal. Transmuting one with a thorium bar produces arcanite, giving a load of mined metal a very different destination.

The crystals also go into the Cap of the Scarlet Savant. They need not become metal to find work among a spellcaster's possessions.

## 12364 Huge Emerald

Huge Emeralds have a place in scrying as well as in the smith's work. Geologist Larksbane requests them among the materials for her research in Silithus.

Thorium veins and obsidian chunks can yield them. They also go into Gloves of Spell Mastery, Wildthorn Mail and Dawnbringer Shoulders.

## 12799 Large Opal

Large Opals are found with thorium. The gem deserves attention alongside the metal, even when the expedition set out with only a smelting order.

Smiths use it in an Enchanted Thorium Helm and Stronghold Gauntlets. A good mineral collection and a well-equipped company often begin with the same finds.

## 12800 Azerothian Diamond

An Azerothian Diamond can serve as part of a Divination Scryer. Lord Grayson Shadowbreaker pairs one with a Pristine Black Diamond to pierce the shadows around a fallen charger's soul.

The gem also goes into Lionheart and Whitesoul Helms. A mineral collector may be supplying armour for the living or an instrument concerned with the dead.

## 19774 Souldarite

Hakkari Thorium Veins in Zul'Gurub yield Souldarite. An ordinary thorium round elsewhere is no promise of finding it.

It supplies Bloodsoul and Darksoul metalwork, as well as Bloodvine Goggles. The familiar ore and this less familiar companion have different places in the expedition's plans.

## 249391 Pyrite

Pyrite occurs with several of the earlier metals in this world. It can also come away from a large chunk of amber, an unusual addition to a miner's round.

The League's mineral account keeps it under its own name. Finding a new companion to a familiar ore is reason enough to make a note.

## 249425 Bauxite

Gold and mithril deposits can yield Bauxite in this world. The ooze-covered mithril found underground can carry it too.

A deposit's name gives only part of its contents. This mineral deserves attention even when it was gold that persuaded the traveller to stop.

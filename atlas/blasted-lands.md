A red, dead waste around the Dark Portal, where the orcs first came into the world. The Portal is dark now. Nethergarde Keep, on the northern rise, still watches it with mages and soldiers who would rather be anywhere else.

The ogres of Dreadmaul hold the hills, demons walk the Tainted Scar, and the beasts that live here are the ones nothing else would eat. The road comes south from the Swamp of Sorrows and ends at Nethergarde; beyond it, there is no road.

There is no water fit to drink and no game fit to eat. Bring both.

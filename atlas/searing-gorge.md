A burned country of slag and cinders, worked by Dark Iron slaves around the Cauldron, a great pit at its heart. The Thorium Brotherhood, dwarves who left the Dark Irons, keep a camp here and will trade with anyone who proves useful.

Most travellers come in through Blackrock Mountain, where the gorge meets the Burning Steppes. The rest of the gorge is mines, digs, fire elementals, lava spiders and the Dark Irons' overseers.

The ground is hot underfoot. Mind your boots; the soles will not last.

Elwynn's main road runs from Stormwind through Goldshire and east to Redridge. Northshire occupies a sheltered valley to the north, while the western crossing leads into Westfall. Farms, bridges and lakes give this country unusually clear bearings.

The mines are less orderly than the fields above them: kobolds occupy Fargodeep and Jasperlode, and Defias activity reaches well into the forest. In the south-west, the Riverpaw camps make the open woodland around the river dangerous ground.

Stormwind is never far away in miles. A farmer waiting for help with a mine or a camp may have a different measure of the distance.

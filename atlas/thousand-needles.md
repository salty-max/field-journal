A canyon of stone spires below the Barrens, reached by the Great Lift at its northern rim. Freewind Post, the Horde's, stands on top of a needle with lifts of its own; the road runs down through the canyon to the Shimmering Flats in the east and on to Tanaris.

The Grimtotem hold Darkcloud Pinnacle and its camps, the centaurs hold Splithoof Crag, and the harpies hold the Screeching Canyon, as advertised. On the salt flats, the gnomes and goblins race their machines at the Mirage Raceway, and are always looking for help.

There is no shade on the flats and nothing to drink. Cross them early.

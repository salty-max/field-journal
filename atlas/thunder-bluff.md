The tauren's city on the mesas of northern Mulgore, joined by rope bridges and reached by great lifts from the plain below.

Each rise has its purpose: the elders on one, the spirits on another, the druids and the shamans on theirs. The bank and the auction house are on the central rise, and the wind riders fly from the tower.

The bridges sway. Do not look down, and do not run.

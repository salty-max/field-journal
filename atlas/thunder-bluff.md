The tauren's city on the mesas of northern Mulgore, joined by rope bridges and reached by great lifts from the plain below.

Each rise has its purpose: Elder Rise for the elders and the druids, Spirit Rise for the shamans and the priests, Hunter Rise for the hunters and the warriors. The bank and the auction house are on the central rise, and the wind riders fly from the tower.

The bridges sway. Do not look down, and do not run.

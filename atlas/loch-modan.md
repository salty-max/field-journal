A long lake held back by the Stonewrought Dam, the finest piece of dwarven engineering outside Ironforge, and I will hear no argument. Thelsamar sits on the western shore on the road from Dun Morogh, and the road goes on north to the Wetlands and south to the Badlands.

The Valley of Kings at the pass has stone kings to watch over it. The rest of the country needs watching: troggs in Stonesplinter Valley, kobolds in the mines, and ogres in the Mo'grosh Stronghold in the north-east hills.

The high elves of the Farstrider Lodge keep to themselves and know the country better than any dwarf. Ask them before you go hunting.

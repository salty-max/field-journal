This is where the plague took hold. The fields are rotten, the farms are haunted, and the town of Andorhal, where the plague grain was stored, is a ruin full of the dead. The road enters from Tirisfal at the Bulwark and runs east to the Thondroril River and the worse country beyond it.

The Scarlet Crusade holds Hearthglen in the north. In the south, on an island in Darrowmere Lake, Caer Darrow hides the Scholomance, the Scourge's school of necromancy. The Alliance keeps Chillwind Camp nearby, for those who go there on purpose.

Boil your water. Then boil it again, and drink something else.

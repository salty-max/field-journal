The road enters at the Bulwark and crosses ruined farming country toward the Thondroril River. Andorhal stands near the centre, its roads and broken buildings still marking the old grain town.

Hearthglen remains a Scarlet stronghold in the north. In the south, Caer Darrow occupies an island in Darrowmere Lake, with Scholomance beneath it; Chillwind Camp gives the Alliance a foothold nearby.

The farms are easy to identify and difficult to approach. Their plague cauldrons have made each field a separate piece of hostile ground, where a map of roads must serve a purpose very different from bringing the harvest in.

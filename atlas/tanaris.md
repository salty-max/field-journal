The great desert of the south, and Gadgetzan, the goblins' town, in the middle of it: neutral, walled and guarded by bruisers who will break up any fight inside the walls. The road comes from the Thousand Needles; Steamwheedle Port lies on the eastern coast.

The Sandfury trolls hold Zul'Farrak in the north-west, the pirates hold Lost Rigger Cove, the ogres hold Dunemaul, and the silithid dig in the south. In the eastern mountains, the bronze dragons guard the Caverns of Time, and the Valley of the Watchers hides something older.

Water is sold by the cup in Gadgetzan. Buy more than you think you need.

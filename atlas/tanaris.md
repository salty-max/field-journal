The descent from Thousand Needles reaches Gadgetzan near the northern end of Tanaris. Its walls and Steamwheedle Port on the eastern coast are useful fixed points in a desert whose southern reaches are much less settled.

Zul'Farrak stands in the north-west, Dunemaul ogres occupy the interior, and Lost Rigger Cove shelters pirates behind the eastern hills. The Caverns of Time open among the mountains farther south-east.

The westward route eventually descends into Un'Goro. From the rim, the green crater looks like another country entirely, which is an unusually agreeable discovery after so much sand.

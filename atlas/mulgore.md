Green plains and grazing kodo under a big sky: the tauren's home. Thunder Bluff stands on its mesas in the north; Bloodhoof Village sits in the south, and the road runs between them and out east to the Barrens.

The plains are kind, mostly. The water wells are the tauren's, and worth guarding; the gnolls of Palemane Rock, the harpies of Windfury Ridge and the Venture Company's mine are not. The dwarves at the Bael'dun digsite dig where the tauren would rather they did not.

Red Rocks, in the east, is a resting place of the tauren's honoured dead. Walk there quietly.

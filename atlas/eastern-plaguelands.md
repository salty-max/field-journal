The worst country in the world, and I have walked the Blasted Lands. The Thondroril River marks its edge; beyond it the trees are dead, the earth is sick and the plague's own city, Stratholme, stands in the north-west, burned and still full.

Light's Hope Chapel in the east is the Argent Dawn's, and the only safe roof here. The Scarlet Crusade holds Tyr's Hand in the south-east; the Mossflayer trolls hold Zul'Mashar; and above the Plaguewood, these days, a dark citadel hangs in the sky.

Darrowshire is a ghost town in the plainest sense. Some of the ghosts will talk to you. Listen to them.

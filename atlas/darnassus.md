zone: 1457

Darnassus opens around water and broad bridges high in Teldrassil. The Temple of the Moon gives the southern part of the city its great landmark, while traders and craftspeople occupy the terraces farther north.

The road east leads to Dolanaar. At the city's western side, the portal provides passage down to Rut'theran Village and the boats below. A surveyor used to measuring a city's height from the ground has some adjusting to do here.

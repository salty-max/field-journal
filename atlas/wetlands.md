Wet is the word. The road from Loch Modan comes down through the pass of Dun Algaz into fens and marshes that never quite dry, and runs west to Menethil Harbor on the coast and north to the great bridge of Thandol Span.

Menethil is the Alliance's port in the north: ships to Auberdine and to Theramore, and an inn full of people waiting for them. Inland, the Dragonmaw orcs hold the hills, the Dark Irons hold Dun Modr in the north, and in the east the gates of Grim Batol are shut and best left that way.

The marshes are murloc and crocolisk country. Watch your footing, and watch the water you are footing in.

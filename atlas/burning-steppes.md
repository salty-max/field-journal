Ash and fire, the country south of Blackrock Mountain. The mountain itself stands on the northern edge, the Firelord in its heart and the black dragons at its top; the Blackrock orcs hold its stronghold, and their worgs and ogres hold the plain.

The road comes up from Redridge through Render's Valley. Morgan's Vigil, on the eastern cliffs, is the Alliance's foothold; it is a small one. The black dragonflight nests at Draco'dar, and its whelps are everywhere.

There is nothing to eat here that will not try to eat you first. Bring supplies.

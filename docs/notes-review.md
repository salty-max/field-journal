# Review of the naturalist's notes (5 October 2026)

All 96 notes checked against the original game, with the Codex's scope: lore up
to the original game, from sources published before Wrath of the Lich King.

## Method

- **Where creatures live**: every place a note names was checked against the
  spawn points of the family's own creatures (CMaNGOS Classic database, the
  zones' map rectangles), creature by creature.
- **Lore**: the Warcraft Wiki, dating each claim (the sources of the time: the
  game's texts, Warcraft III, the novels and the RPG books before November 2008).
- **Names**: every named kind (stagwings, fallout, thunderers, rumblers...)
  checked to exist in the original game.

Lore:

- **Burning Legion**: "came for Azeroth three times" became "twice in force, and
  once more through the orcs it corrupted".
- **Troggs**: the titans' records (the Uldaman discs; Horde Player's Guide,
  2006) call the troggs the makers' first, failed attempt at life from stone,
  buried in vaults; not "the earthen gone wrong".
- **Worgen of Duskwood**: the hint at an elven relic (from later stories) removed.
- **Fire elementals**: Ragnaros woken "more than" two hundred years ago (the War
  of the Three Hammers).
- **Jungle trolls**: the sunken temple is in the Swamp of Sorrows, and the Atal'ai
  were cast out by the Gurubashi empire.
- **Blue dragonflight**: the unsourced "matron" of Winterspring became the ice
  caves of Mazthoril.

Unsourced details replaced:

- **Insects**: a legend of Zul'Farrak's scarabs and a martyred troll (invented).
- **Sand trolls**: a Sandfury tempering agent prized in Gadgetzan (invented);
  now the Shimmering Flats' trade in Gahz'rilla's scales (the original game's
  quest).
- **Wildkin**: a scholar of Starfall Village (invented); now what the night
  elves say of them.
- **Tallstriders**: "a smaller, shyer cousin" in Darkshore (the foreststriders
  are no smaller).

## Confirmed

Everything else, including: the kodo graveyard of Desolace; the Marshal
expedition in Un'Goro; Xavius as the first satyr; Zaetar and Theradras and the
five khans; Naralex and the Druids of the Fang; the Stonemasons and VanCleef;
the Dragonmaw at Grim Batol; the Shadow Council over the Burning Blade and in
Jaedenar; the Dark Strand; the Syndicate and Alterac; the Lost Ones as draenei
from the orcs' world (the original game's Draenei Refugees of the Blasted Lands);
the furbolg tribes and the Timbermaw; every gnoll, harpy, ogre and troll tribe
and where it lives.

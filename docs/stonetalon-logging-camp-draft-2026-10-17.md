# Stonetalon logging camp: sixth requested addition

Requested on 17 October 2026 alongside the five additions now implemented.
English working text only. Not loaded by the addon, not translated and not
included in expedition completion totals.

## Why it is held

The scene is supported, but its solution is not ready. wow-data has no
creature, object or narrow named area identifying the actual mill yard.
The 2010 podcast's **37, 34** is an approximate destination, not a measured
solution footprint. It must not become a fabricated object or exact arrival
coordinate in Yonder.

Access also needs resolving. A [firsthand original-era route](https://www.wowhead.com/tbc/zone=406/stonetalon-mountains#comments)
describes climbing beside Talon Den and jumping onto its roof to pass the
ridge. A [Classic-era discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/classicwow/comments/n9ks0j)
describes wall jumping and clipping past an invisible wall. Neither proves
the ordinary walking route required for this batch. The linked Lady Erinia
route in the podcast index now returns HTTP 410.

## Proposed text

Suggested id: `stonetalon-mountains-silent-mill`. Tier: hidden. Arrival only,
outside combat as usual. No working machinery, live crew or emote required.

> West of the mountain road, the timber has stopped moving.
> Cranes still wait above a mill without its crew.
> Find the yard where the trees were brought down,
> and the work was left standing.

Paid hint: The abandoned logging camp in the western mountains, away from
Windshear Crag. Reach the mill yard. No gesture is needed.

Solved note: An abandoned logging camp in Stonetalon's western mountains.
Cranes and a lumber mill remain, but there is no crew at work.

Gold margin: Three landing platforms stand on the heights, two still
occupied by flying machines. The third is an empty berth, not an account
of where its owner went.

These scene details come from the pre-Cataclysm
[logging-camp account](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/West_of_Stonetalon_Mountains).
The [2010 exploration podcast index](https://toppodcast.com/podcast_feeds/warcraft-less-traveled-a-world-of-warcraft-podcast/)
provides an independent destination lead, and
[Madburst's 2020 itinerary](https://www.warcraftmovies.com/movieview.php?id=252884)
includes a Classic visit at 02:34. None establishes a current Forever route.
Do not invent a Venture Company owner, a disaster, or a connection to a
crashed aircraft elsewhere.

## What finishes it

1. Establish ordinary access on each game. If it depends on crossing an
   invisible wall or geometry exploit, exclude it under the existing policy.
2. Observe the mill yard and record a bounded footprint, with elevation if
   another surface lies beneath it. Carry a sourced landmark record through
   wow-data, without representing an observer's position as a game object.
3. Reconcile the verse and hint with that actual route, review the notes,
   then translate into French, German and Spanish.
4. Exercise the arrival through the normal event loop and all client
   languages. Reject nearby ridges, flying over the camp and unrelated mills.

# The Atlas: voice sample

The surveyor of the Explorers' League: a dwarf, Ironforge-trained, who has
walked every road in these pages with a chain, a compass and a bad knee. Where
the naturalist writes about what lives in a place, the surveyor writes about
the place itself: the lie of the land, the roads and where they go, water,
shelter, the dangers of the ground and of its people. Short, practical, dry.
Same rules as the Bestiary: lore up to the original game, from sources before
Wrath of the Lich King, plain ASCII, nothing invented that a traveller could
check and find false.

---

## Elwynn Forest

The gentlest country in the Eastern Kingdoms, and the best mapped: the King's road runs from the gates of Stormwind through Goldshire and on east to Redridge, and every farm along it has been surveyed, taxed and argued over. Northshire Abbey sits in its own valley to the north, where Stormwind trains its young.

Water is never far: Crystal Lake, Stone Cairn Lake, Mirror Lake and the river that marks the Westfall border. The danger is underground. The kobolds have the Jasperlode and Fargodeep mines, and the Defias have more of the forest's farms than the Crown likes to admit.

Keep off the road south-west of Goldshire after dark. Something large lives in the woods there, and the guards have stopped going to look.

## Dun Morogh

Snow, stone and dwarves, in that order. The valleys run east and west below the peaks that hide Ironforge, and the one road follows them: from Coldridge Valley through the tunnel to Kharanos, up to the city's great gate, and on east through the pass to Loch Modan.

Kharanos has the Thunderbrew distillery and the best fire in the land. West of it the ground belongs to the Frostmane trolls, and further west Gnomeregan stands behind its walls, poisoned; the gnomes do not go home, and you should not go in without good reason and better friends.

Carry a spare cloak and keep to the road in a snowstorm. The drifts are deep, and the troggs of Coldridge do not care that you are lost.

## The Barrens

A great dry plain with one crossroads in the middle of it, and the Crossroads knows it. Every road meets there: north to Ashenvale past the Mor'shan Rampart, east to Ratchet and the sea, south through Camp Taurajo to the Great Lift and the Thousand Needles, west into the hills of Stonetalon.

Water is the whole question. The oases are few and fouled, the wells are guarded, and the grass hides more than it shows: quilboar in the thorn-mounds, centaurs on the horizon, and in the south the bramble-walls of Razorfen, which you will want to see once and never again.

Ratchet's boat goes to Booty Bay, and the goblins will take anyone's money. Fill your waterskin before you leave the Crossroads, whatever the innkeeper charges.

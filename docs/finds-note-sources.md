# Material note sources

English pass, 16 October 2026. Acquisition and direct crafting uses are
checked through `scripts/materials.py` against wow-data's Classic and
Forever databases. Its ignored `.cache/materials-evidence.json` contains
the item, source and recipe identities. This file records the additional
textual evidence behind the notes, rather than shipping the research.

The quest references below are the game's English text preserved in
wow-data's `quest_text`, with identities, objectives and speakers from
`name`, `quest_objective` and `quest_relation`. The original quest prose
comes from the community database reconstruction. The referenced quests
also have identities in the Forever database, and were checked there
alongside the Classic account. That is not proof of a quest being currently
obtainable on the beta. Notes do not promise that any quest is available
to the reader.

## Ores and gems

| Item | Evidence and limits |
| --- | --- |
| Copper Ore 2770 | Quest 1578, *Supplying the Front*: copper axes and chain belts for Loch Modan's mountaineers. Spell 2659 combines copper and tin bars into bronze. |
| Tin Ore 2771 | Spell 2659, *Smelt Bronze*. The note describes the alloy, without inventing a property of the raw ore. |
| Silver Ore 2775 | Spells 2658, 7818 and 7795: ore to bar, silver rod, then runed silver rod. |
| Iron Ore 2772 | Spell 3569, *Smelt Steel*: iron bar and coal. Spell 13612, *Enchant Gloves - Mining*, uses the ore. |
| Gold Ore 2776 | Spell 14379, *Golden Rod*, uses Gold Bar. Quest 4083, *The Spectral Chalice*, requires the bars. The note distinguishes ore from the smelted offering. |
| Mithril Ore 3858 and Truesilver Ore 7911 | Quests 2760-2762, *The Mithril Order*, *Smelt On, Smelt Off* and *The Great Silver Deceiver*. Galvan accepts truesilver's greater strength and durability but prefers mithril. His judgment of nobility stays an opinion. |
| Thorium Ore 10620 | Quest 312, *Tundra MacGrann's Stolen Stash*: MacGrann trusts his thorium meat locker to withstand the bear, which has carried it away. The note attributes his belief, rather than declaring the metal unbreakable. |
| Dark Iron Ore 11370 | Original-world recipes using Dark Iron Bar. Quest 7627, *Wheel of the Black March*, describes the wheel and its summoning purpose. [Smelt Dark Iron 14891](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=14891/smelt-dark-iron), the game's tooltip reproduced by Wowhead, specifies the Black Forge in Blackrock Depths. |
| Rethban Ore 2798 | Quest 347, *Rethban Ore*: discoverer Bart Rethban, low strength when smelted, dark ore used for scribes' dye. |
| Incendicite Ore 3340 | Quest 466, *Search for Incendicite*: volatile ore. Cannon shells are Stonegear's proposed experiment, not an established application. |
| Lesser Bloodstone Ore 4278 | Quest 627, *Favor for Krazek*: low-grade bloodstone from Drywhisker Gorge, requested while Krazek repairs a cooking pot. Belamoore's research concerns other bloodstone specimens, so the note does not transfer her conclusions to this item. |
| Indurium Ore 5833 | Quest 1192, *Indurium Ore*, request and reward: Uldaman ore for parts of racing vehicles. |
| Moss Agate 1206 | Quest 2950, *Nogg's Ring Redo*: Nogg adds a silver and agate lattice to strengthen the ring's magic. |
| Jade 1529 | Quest 1948, *Items of Power*: one of Tabetha's required materials for a wand. The vessel must hold magical energy. The note does not claim jade alone stores or generates that energy. |
| Citrine 3864 | Quest 2763, [*The Art of the Imbue*](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=2763/the-art-of-the-imbue): Galvan describes amplification of enchantments. |
| Star Ruby 7910 | Quest 4083, *The Spectral Chalice*: an actual offering, distinct from a claim that rubies have an intrinsic connection to spirits. |
| Black Diamond 11754 | Quests 4463 and 4481-4484: Mathredis Firestar's librams and lesser arcana. These require Black Diamond. The separate Pristine Black Diamond used by Dire Maul's librams is not substituted. |
| Arcane Crystal 12363 | Spell 17187, *Transmute: Arcanite*. Quest 5063, *Cap of the Scarlet Savant*, also requires crystals. |
| Huge Emerald 12364 | Quests 8779 and 8807, *Scrying Materials*: Geologist Larksbane's research supplies. The gem's presence among the reagents does not establish an independent power to scry. |
| Azerothian Diamond 12800 | Quest 7646, *The Divination Scryer*: paired with a Pristine Black Diamond for the fallen charger's redemption. |

## Cloth and leather

| Item | Evidence and limits |
| --- | --- |
| Linen Cloth 2589 | Quest 1644, *The Tome of Divinity*: Stephanie Turner's clothing for children orphaned by the Scourge. Linen bandages are spells 3275 and 3276. |
| Wool Cloth 2592 | Quest 388, *The Color of Blood*: Stockade prisoners tear wool blankets into Defias bandanas. Wool bandages are spells 3277 and 3278. |
| Silk Cloth 4306 | Quest 214, *Red Silk Bandanas*: the Defias bandanas' material indicates rank. Silk bandages are spells 7928 and 7929. This is not evidence that ordinary Silk Cloth comes from a particular spider species. |
| Mageweave Cloth 4338 | Spell 3865 makes bolts. Dreamweave spells 12067, 12070 and 12092 use those bolts and Wildvine. Quests 2847 and 2854 describe Wildvine's qualities. The note attributes those qualities to the plant, not to raw Mageweave. Bandages are spells 10840 and 10841. |
| Runecloth 14047 | Quest 6031, *Runecloth*: Meilosh's people admire its feel and a visitor's cloak. Quest 7642, *Collection of Goods*: lining for the charger's barding. Bandages are spells 18629 and 18630. |
| Felcloth 14256 | Quests 8419 and 8420: Impsy's description of contact with the fabric stays attributed to him. Spell 18560 makes Mooncloth from Felcloth. No unsupported account of where the original fibres grew. |
| Ruined Leather Scraps 2934 | Spell 2881 joins scraps into Light Leather. |
| Light Leather 2318 | Quests 768 and 769: Veren Tallstrider's work for Thunder Bluff and his Kodo Hide Bag pattern. This does not imply that all Light Leather is kodo skin. |
| Heavy Leather 4234 | Quest 8588, details only: armour, reinforcement and tarpaulins. Its unrelated reward text is not used. Quest 8242: a supply for fiery flux with Incendosaur Scales and coal. |
| Thick Leather 4304 | Quest 8516: steam tanks, rifle scopes and goggles. Quest 8590, reward: zeppelin reinforcement and equipment. These are uses, not evidence of a magical property. |
| Rugged Leather 8170 | Quest 5518, *The Gordok Ogre Suit*: Knot Thimblejack's temporary disguise, its instability and Captain Kromcrush. The instability belongs to the suit, not to Rugged Leather generally. |
| Light, Medium and Heavy Hide 783, 4232, 4235 | Curing spells 3816-3818 use ordinary Salt. Heavy Hide needs more salt than the lighter kinds. |
| Thick Hide 8169 | Curing spell 10482 uses Deeprock Salt. |
| Rugged Hide 8171 | Curing spell 19047 uses Refined Deeprock Salt. |

Recipe ingredients for the alloy, curing, dreamweave and Mooncloth claims
were compared between both client databases. No chemical, magical or
protective property has been inferred merely from a finished item's name
or statistics. Entries with sparse lore keep their confirmed practical
uses rather than acquiring invented histories.

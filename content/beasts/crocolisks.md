Crocolisks are ancient things, long-jawed and armoured, which have outlasted empires by the simple method of lying still in warm water and eating whatever comes to drink. They haunt the lochs of Khaz Modan, the bogs of the Wetlands, the rivers of Stranglethorn and the shores of Dustwallow.

A crocolisk on the bank looks asleep. It is not. It can cover the distance to a careless traveller faster than seems decent for something so heavy.

Their hides make fine leather, and their meat is better than it has any right to be. Neither is worth losing a leg over.

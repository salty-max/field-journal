The great cats are the quietest killers in this book. The nightsabers of Teldrassil, the frostsabers of Winterspring, the panthers and tigers of Stranglethorn and the mountain lions of the Alterac foothills all share one habit: you rarely see them until they have already decided about you.

The night elves ride the sabers and honour them; the trolls of the south hunt the jungle cats for their pelts and their courage. Either way, a cat that has marked you will stalk you a long time, and it will choose the moment, not you.

Watch the long grass. Then watch it again.

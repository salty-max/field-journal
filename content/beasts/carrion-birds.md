The carrion birds are the patient ones: vultures, buzzards and condors circling over the Barrens, the Badlands and the Thousand Needles, waiting for the land to do their killing for them. The fleshrippers of Westfall have grown bolder since the farms emptied, and will not always wait.

They are a sign worth reading. A ring of them in the sky means something below has died or is about to, and sometimes it is wise to find out what before you walk into it.

The rocs of the far south are another matter: great birds of prey that hunt living game, and treat a traveller as such.

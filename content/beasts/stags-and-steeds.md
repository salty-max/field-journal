The grazers of the open lands are many and mostly peaceful: the zhevras and giraffes of the Barrens, the stags of Ashenvale and Feralas, the coursers of the grasslands, and the wild horses that roam where the farms have been abandoned.

They are the game of every hunter, from tauren to troll to the Kaldorei who thank the forest for each one taken. Most will flee rather than fight; a stag in the rut, or a mare with a foal, may not.

The stagwings of Feralas fly, which is not a sentence I expected to write in a natural history.

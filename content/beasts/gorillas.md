The gorillas of Stranglethorn, Feralas and Un'Goro have the bulk to be noticed in a crowded jungle. Silverbacks, mistvale apes and the great thunderers differ in size, but even the smaller ones make the tree trunks around them look slender.

The names tell something of the impression they leave: King Mukla, Uhk'loc, U'cha. Hunters go looking for a particular beast and come back with a name that sounds like a ruler's. A gorilla needs neither crown nor throne to occupy a clearing very thoroughly.

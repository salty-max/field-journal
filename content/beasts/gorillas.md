The great apes of Stranglethorn and Feralas live in troops on the high ground, led by the largest silverback, and they are a good deal cleverer than the jungle trolls who hunt them like to admit. The gorillas of the Mistvale and the thunderers of the jungle beat their chests and the trees around them before a fight, which gives fair warning to anyone who listens.

They are not cruel, only territorial, and a troop's patience ends at its feeding grounds.

There are tales of kings among them, larger than any troll and treated with a respect a troll chieftain might envy.

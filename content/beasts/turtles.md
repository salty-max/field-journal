A turtle's shell makes the rest of the creature seem an afterthought until the head emerges. Snapjaws inhabit lakes and rivers; the large coastal turtles of Tanaris and Azshara carry the same sturdy design into salt water.

Their patience is easy to admire from the bank. Up close, the beak deserves equal study. Between the two sits an animal remarkably well equipped to occupy a stretch of water without appearing to exert itself.

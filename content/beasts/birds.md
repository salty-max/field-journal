The great birds of the high places are lords of the cliffs: the wyverns of the Barrens and Stonetalon, the thunderhawks of the plains, the hippogryphs of Azshara and Feralas, and the wild gryphons of the Hinterlands that the Wildhammer have not yet taken in hand.

The wyverns and hippogryphs carry the riders of the Horde and the night elves; their wild kin carry nobody, and defend their nests against anything that climbs.

The firebirds of Tanaris do not look like any bird I know, and they are hot to stand near.

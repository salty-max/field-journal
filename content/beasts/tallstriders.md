The tallstriders are long-legged, long-necked birds of the open plains, and the plainstriders of Mulgore and the Barrens are the most familiar sight of the grasslands after the kodo. They are harmless enough until cornered, and then they kick hard enough to break a shield.

The tauren hunt them for their meat and feathers, with the thanks the Earth Mother asks of them; the night elves of Darkshore and Felwood know a forest cousin, the foreststrider.

Their eggs are prized. Their mothers are aware of this.

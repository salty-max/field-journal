Below the waves is another bestiary entirely, and this page covers only the part of it that bites: the sharks of the southern seas, the threshers of Darkshore and Dustwallow, the frenzies that boil in the lochs and rivers at the smell of blood, and the snappers of the warm coasts.

A swimmer bleeding into the water is a dinner bell. The frenzies of the Barrens' oases and Loch Modan are small, but they come in hundreds.

If you must swim, swim fast and swim clean. If you must fish, fish from a boat.

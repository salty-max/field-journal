The burrowers and grubs of Azeroth are the earth's cleaners: maggots in the dead fields of the Plaguelands, borers in the rock of the Badlands and the Thousand Needles, grubs under every corpse left too long. Most are small; some are not.

The rock worms of the deep places can swallow a dwarf, as more than one prospector of the League has learned too late to report.

They are not hunters so much as appetites. That does not make them any safer to stand on.

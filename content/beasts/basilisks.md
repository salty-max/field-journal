Basilisks are heavy, low-slung lizards with stony hides and stonier stares, found in the hot and barren places: the hills of Stranglethorn, Tanaris, Desolace, the Thousand Needles, the Blasted Lands. Their gaze is said to turn flesh to stone, and while I have not seen it happen, I have seen enough of their victims stiff and grey to keep my eyes down.

Crystals grow on the backs of the older ones, which tells you something about how long they lie still.

The Explorers' League would like very much to know what they eat. Mostly, it appears, explorers.

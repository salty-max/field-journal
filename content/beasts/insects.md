Beyond the silithid, which have their own pages, Azeroth's insects are mostly beneath a traveller's notice: beetles, flies, the scarabs that scuttle in the dry places. The scarabs of the south are another matter, large enough to fight and old enough in their habits to seem deliberate.

They gather around the dead, and in Zul'Farrak around the shrine of a martyred troll, where the Sandfury say the insects were once his killers.

Watch the scarabs. They are smaller than the silithid, and they come from the same deserts.

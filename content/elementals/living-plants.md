Some of Azeroth's forests fight back. Timberlings and treants, lashers and creepers, the ancients of the night elves' woods and the sprites that dance among them: plants with will, and sometimes with malice.

Many of them were gentle once. The satyrs' corruption, the Legion's taint and the poisons of the Plaguelands have turned whole groves against the travellers who walk them. The swamp creatures of the Wetlands, Dustwallow and the Swamp of Sorrows are another kind: things of mud and rot that walk.

The druids will tell you which can be saved. Listen to them before you burn anything.

Air elementals are storms with intent: dust devils over the Barrens and Westfall, whirlwinds in Desolace and Silithus, cyclones that walk the high places. In Silithus the Twilight's Hammer calls up their lords, which is as bad an idea as it sounds.

They are hard to see, hard to hit and easy to underestimate, until one lifts you off your feet.

Prince Thunderaan, a lord of the air, lost his essence to the Firelord long ago, and what is left of him is a story for another page.

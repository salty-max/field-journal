Dust devils cross open fields, while greater whirlwinds take shape in Desolace and Silithus. An air elemental is easiest to see in what it gathers around itself: dust, cloud and the loose things a traveller thought securely packed.

The Twilight's Hammer summons elemental powers in the southern desert. A storm encountered there may therefore have more behind it than unsettled weather. The wind has acquired a shape, and somebody may have invited it.

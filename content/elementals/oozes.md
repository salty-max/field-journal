Oozes, slimes and sludges collect wherever there is filth and damp: in Dustwallow's bogs, the sewers of the Undercity, the swamps of Sorrows, the radiation of Gnomeregan and the poisoned waters of the Plaguelands. They are less creatures than appetites with a shape.

They dissolve what they swallow, armour included, and they divide when struck carelessly.

Do not step in anything that moves. It is a good rule in general and a vital one here.

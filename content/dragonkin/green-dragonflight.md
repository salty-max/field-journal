The green dragons are Ysera's, the Dreamer's, guardians of the Emerald Dream, and something has gone wrong with them. Some of Ysera's flight are lost in nightmares: Eranikus over the sunken temple, the dragonkin at the great trees of Duskwood, the Hinterlands, Feralas and Ashenvale.

Their whelps and wyrmkin dream as they fight, and the Nightmare dreams through them.

If a green dragon speaks to you of sleep, do not listen too closely.

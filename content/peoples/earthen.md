The earthen are beings of living stone, made by the titans to shape the deep places of the world, and in Uldaman some of them still walk the halls of the titans' vault, guarding it as they were made to.

The platinum discs in that vault say the dwarves descend from them. These are, in the plainest sense, my ancestors, and they will try to kill you.

I record them with a respect I cannot quite explain, and a caution I can.

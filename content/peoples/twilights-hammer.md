The Twilight's Hammer was an orc clan of the old Horde that believed the world should end, and it has become a cult of every race with the same belief. Its people gather at idols in Blackfathom Deeps, Darkshore, the Searing Gorge and the deserts of Silithus, where they call up elemental lords.

They worship powers older than the Legion and darker than the dead, and their tracts are mostly about the end of all things.

They seem to look forward to it. Do not let them hurry it along.

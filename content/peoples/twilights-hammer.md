The Twilight's Hammer has grown from an orc clan into a cult with followers of many peoples. Its camps gather around old powers: in Blackfathom Deeps, near dark idols, and across the elemental country of Silithus.

The cult's writings look forward to the end of the world, and its summoners work to hasten it. Robes and scholarly trappings can give the camps an appearance of study. The summoned elemental lords explain what the studies are for.

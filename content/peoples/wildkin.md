The wildkin are great feathered beasts, owl and bear at once, held sacred by the night elves as creatures of Elune. The moonkin of Darkshore are gentle until provoked; the owlbeasts of the Hinterlands and Winterspring are wilder and have not the same patience.

The night elves say they are not beasts at all, but creatures of the goddess. Having watched one stand guard in the moonlight, I am inclined to agree.

Approach with respect. Leave with all your feathers, as it were.

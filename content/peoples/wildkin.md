A wildkin has the bulk of a bear beneath an owl's face and feathers, a combination that deserves a quiet moment of attention. The night elves hold these creatures sacred to Elune.

Moonkin live in Darkshore, and owlbeasts occupy the Hinterlands and Winterspring. Their names and temperaments vary, but the great round eyes remain distinctive. A forest containing one can feel watched in a way that has little to do with a hunter's ambush.

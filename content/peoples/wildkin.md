The wildkin are great feathered beasts, owl and bear at once, held sacred by the night elves as creatures of Elune. The moonkin of Darkshore are gentle until provoked; the owlbeasts of the Hinterlands, Felwood and Winterspring are wilder and have not the same patience.

A scholar of Starfall Village believes they are not beasts at all, but creatures of the goddess. Having watched one stand guard over a moonwell, I am inclined to agree.

Approach with respect. Leave with all your feathers, as it were.

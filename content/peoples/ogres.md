The ogres came to this world with the Horde, from the orcs' own red land, and when the Horde broke they scattered into the wild in clans of their own: the Mo'grosh of Loch Modan, the Splinter Fist of Duskwood, the Crushridge of Alterac, the Boulderfist of Arathi, the Dustbelcher of the Badlands, the Mosh'Ogg of Stranglethorn, the Gordunni of Feralas and many more.

Huge, brutal and cleverer than they look, especially the two-headed ogre magi. Their shamans and warlocks are a danger out of proportion to their numbers.

The Stonemaul of Dustwallow fight beside the Horde. The rest would rather eat you than talk.

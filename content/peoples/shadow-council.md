The Shadow Council helped bind the old Horde to the Legion. Its surviving influence now appears in places such as Jaedenar, where cultists and demons occupy the same corrupted ground.

Thrall's Horde has rejected those masters, but the summoning circles remain. Around them gather practitioners from more than one people, pursuing arts whose cost is already written across Felwood. The blackened trees are a useful accompaniment to any promises made inside the camp.

An elven expedition among the ruins may be seeking the same things as the League: an inscription, a relic, a surviving piece of ancient craft. In Azshara, the blood elves' pursuit of lost arcane power makes that common interest a poor guarantee of welcome.

Dire Maul has its own dangerous students of magic, Lethtendris among them. The old cities still contain power worth seeking, and the people who reach it do not always leave room for a second scholar.

Not every elf in Azeroth is a friend of the Alliance. In Azshara, blood elves of Quel'Thalas have come looking for the Highborne's lost magic among the ruins, and they defend their finds with spells. In Dire Maul, Lethtendris and others feed on the arcane power still trapped in the city.

They are few, clever and very dangerous to anyone carrying a spellbook.

The night elves of Darnassus regard them with a suspicion ten thousand years old. Having met them, I find the suspicion well founded.

A red mask can mean a cutpurse on an Elwynn road or a craftsman at work beneath the Deadmines. The Defias Brotherhood grew out of the Stonemasons' unpaid labour rebuilding Stormwind, and Edwin VanCleef carried that grievance into Westfall.

The farms now suffer for a quarrel their owners did not settle. Under Moonbrook, the Brotherhood has organised something far larger than roadside robbery: workshops, hired muscle and a ship. The masons' skill has survived; the city it was meant to serve has become their target.

Kobolds are small, rat-faced diggers of the tunnels, and they are everywhere there is earth to dig: the mines of Elwynn and Westfall, the hills above Loch Modan, the Arathi Highlands and the canyons of Thousand Needles. Each wears a candle on its head, and will fight harder for that candle than for its life.

Alone they are cowards; in numbers they are a plague. Miners curse them, farmers lose cattle to them, and every young soldier of Stormwind learns to fight on them.

"You no take candle!" is the only thing most travellers ever hear one say. It is, I have found, the whole of their philosophy.

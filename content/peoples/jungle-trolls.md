Stranglethorn's troll ruins are inhabited history. Bloodscalp and Skullsplitter tribes fight among the old stones, while Zul'Gurub remains a centre of Gurubashi power. Its high priests have brought Hakkar back into the world.

The Atal'ai carry the blood god's worship into the Sunken Temple as well. Other jungle trolls have taken another course: the Darkspear belong to Thrall's Horde, and Yojamba Isle shelters Zandalar's effort against Hakkar. An old empire has left many heirs, with very different purposes.

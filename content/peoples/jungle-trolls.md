The jungle trolls of Stranglethorn are the heirs of the Gurubashi empire, whose ruins fill that jungle: the Bloodscalp and the Skullsplitter fight over them, the Atal'ai, whom the empire cast out, serve the blood god in the sunken temple of the Swamp of Sorrows, and in Zul'Gurub the high priests have brought Hakkar back into the world.

They raid, they take heads, and they hate each other almost as much as they hate everyone else.

The Darkspear are their cousins, and the only jungle trolls in the world who will share a fire with you.

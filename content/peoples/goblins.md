Most goblins a traveller meets are selling something, and most of the rest are working for someone who is. This page holds the ones who are trying to kill you instead: the engineers and craftsmen of the Deadmines who build VanCleef's ship, the tinkerers who have set up shop in dangerous places, and the odd mad genius with a grudge.

They fight with whatever they have built, which is usually explosive, and whatever they have hired, which is usually large.

The Venture Company has a page of its own. These are the freelancers.

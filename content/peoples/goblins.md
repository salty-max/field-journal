The goblins recorded here are encountered across a workshop floor or a battlefield rather than a shop counter. VanCleef has engineers in the Deadmines, and dangerous inventions turn up wherever their makers find room to work.

A shredder or a hired guard tells something about the resources behind a small goblin camp. Much of the fighting may be done by something the engineer has built, bought or persuaded to stand in front. The inventor's stature gives a poor measure of the size of the enterprise.

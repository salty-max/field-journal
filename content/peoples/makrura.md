Makrura occupy Kalimdor's shallows with a presence quite different from the crabs beside them. The upright bodies, grasping claws and guarded colonies belong to a people, however unfamiliar their speech and shape may be.

Durotar's shore gives many young orcs their first encounter with them. Larger colonies lie farther down the coast. A beach offers little cover to a traveller approaching from land, while the water behind a makrura settlement need not be the limit of its inhabitants.

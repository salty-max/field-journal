The makrura are crab-folk of the coasts and lakes, armoured and clawed, living in colonies along the shores and shallows of Kalimdor: Durotar and the Barrens, Desolace and Feralas, Dustwallow and Azshara. They are cleverer than crabs and a good deal angrier.

Their shells are thick and their claws can break a shield. In groups they come scuttling sideways at a speed that seems unfair.

The orcs of Durotar learn on them, and learn to respect them.
